Fruit SlashFinger & Touch Swipe

Swipe through falling fruit to slice it — with your mouse, a finger on a touchscreen, or by pointing at the screen. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate.
  • Swipe through fruit — or tap a single piece — to slice it.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
