Color Quest!Point at the Color

Point your index finger at the shape whose color matches the question, and hold it there for a moment to pick it. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate (skipped automatically if you've already calibrated another app on this wall).
  • Point at, and hold over, the shape with the color being asked for.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
