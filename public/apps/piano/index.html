MyPianoFinger & Touch Piano

Point at, click, or tap piano keys to play them — chords and two-handed playing both work. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate.
  • Point at (or click/tap) a key to play it.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
