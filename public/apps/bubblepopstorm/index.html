Bubble Pop StormTouch-Wall Edition

Pop the rising bubbles with either hand — no holding still, just touch and go. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking (both hands!) — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate (skipped automatically if you've already calibrated another app on this wall).
  • Touch bubbles with either hand to pop them.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
