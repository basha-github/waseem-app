Animal LanguagesFinger & Touch Pointer

Point at, click, or tap a language circle to hear an animal's name spoken in English, Hindi, or Telugu. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate.
  • Point at (or click/tap) a language circle to hear it.
  • Hold over — or click/tap — Next/Prev to change animals.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
