Garden Languages 1 🥕Touch-Wall Edition

Point at, or click/tap, a language circle to hear this item's name spoken in English, Hindi, or Telugu. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate (skipped automatically if you've already calibrated another app on this wall).
  • Point at (or click/tap) a language circle to hear it.
  • Hold over — or click/tap — Next/Prev to change items.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
