Fruit & Veggie SafariTouch-Wall Edition

Point your index finger at the black & white photo — or at Back / Say it again / Next — and hold it there for a moment to select it. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate (skipped automatically if you've already calibrated another app on this wall).
  • Point at, and hold over, the picture or a button to select it.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
