My MusicFinger & Touch Instrument Poster

Point at — or hover the mouse over, or touch — an instrument on the poster to play it. Uses your webcam and on-device hand tracking; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your fingertip still over each of the 9 dots for 2 seconds to calibrate.
  • Point at an instrument to play it — no click needed.
  • Press R any time to recalibrate.
